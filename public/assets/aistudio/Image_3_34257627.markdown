## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an institutional, high-precision aesthetic tailored for rigorous quality assurance, academic governance, and institutional incident auditing. The interface balances executive seriousness with analytical clarity, communicating neutrality, meticulous organization, and authoritative oversight.

The visual direction combines **Corporate Modern** with **Technical Precision**:
- High-density data presentation without visual clutter.
- Low-contrast structural discipline relying on precise 1px architectural lines rather than excessive shadows.
- Calibrated, restrained status signaling to prevent sensory overload during long audit cycles.
- An environment designed for institutional stakeholders, compliance officers, and academic directors requiring immediate operational diagnosis and audit resolution.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an architectural slate framework punctuated by purposeful, restrained semantic accents.

### Core Surfaces & Grayscale
- **Canvas Base:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) provides an anti-glare, pristine base that minimizes eye fatigue during extended auditing sessions.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** `#ffffff` (Pure White) creates crisp spatial separation from the canvas.
- **Surface Level 2 (Sub-panels, Table Headers, Filter Bars):** `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100).
- **Structural Outlines & Dividers:** `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) for standard layout grids; `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300) for interactive control borders.
- **Secondary Text & Meta Information:** `#64748b` (Slate 500) and `#475569` (Slate 600).
- **Body & Header Typography:** `#1e293b` (Slate 800) for primary copy; `#0f172a` (Slate 900) for primary headings and strong structural accents.

### Semantic & Status Tokens
Status indicators use muted, desaturated tones to maintain an executive atmosphere:
- **Primary / Brand Action:** `#1d4ed8` (Corporate Navy Blue). Deep, institutional, and legible against white backgrounds.
- **Resolved / Compliant (Tertiary):** `#059669` (Muted Emerald) paired with a `#ecfdf5` background tint and `#a7f3d0` border.
- **In Progress / Under Review:** `#d97706` (Restrained Amber) paired with a `#fffbeb` background tint and `#fde68a` border.
- **Critical Incidents / Non-Compliant:** `#dc2626` (Muted Burgundy/Red) paired with a `#fef2f2` background tint and `#fecaca` border.
- **Informational / Unassigned:** `#3b82f6` (Cadet Slate Blue) paired with a `#eff6ff` background tint and `#bfdbfe` border.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Inter**, optimized for density and readability across dashboards and tabular views.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tabular Numerals (`tnum`):** All data cells, incident reference counters, percentages, and metrics must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1, "cv08" 1`. This aligns numeric columns vertically without width jitter.
- **Hierarchy by Contrast:** Visual rank is generated by shifting from bold slate titles (`#0f172a`) down to medium muted labels (`#64748b`) rather than relying on oversized point sizes.
- **Micro-Labels:** Badges, operational tags, and metric headers leverage `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`+0.025em`) for legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured **12-column fluid grid system** with rigid gutters and contained max-widths for ultra-wide displays to maintain scannability.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`space-xl`) margins, 24px gutters. Density is maintained at maximum capacity, allowing filter sidebars, tabular grids, and metrics KPI rows to co-exist cleanly.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Filter panels collapse into horizontal drop-drawers or contextual sheet overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters. High-density tables reflow into stacked audit-card summaries showing key status, incident IDs, and urgency.

### Spatial Discipline
The spacing tokens conform to a strict 4px/8px rhythm. Compact interfaces benefit from tight operational padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) inside cell nodes and filter chips, reserving `space-lg` and `space-xl` for sectional structural boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system minimizes dramatic elevation in favor of **structural boundaries and planar tinting**.

### Low-Contrast Structural Framing
- Depth is primarily conveyed through planar contrast (`#f8fafc` canvas vs `#ffffff` cards) framed by crisp 1px borders (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Base):** Flush surfaces with flat hairline dividers. Used for table rows, inline forms, and metric widgets.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Interactive / Raised):** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Applied to active card containers and hoverable rows.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Context Menus):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, bounded by a sharp `#cbd5e1` hairline border.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Audit Modals & Dialogs):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` accompanied by a 40% opacity slate scrim (`#0f172a` at 0.4).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Containers, input fields, and action buttons use clean `0.25rem` (4px) radii, delivering an engineering-grade, structured feel that reinforces reliability and order.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards, Panels):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains sharp lines without becoming aggressively harsh.
- **Surfaces & Overlays (Modals, Slide-over panels):** `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Pill Exception (Status Badges & Count Pills):** Explicitly rounded at `9999px` to create visual contrast against rectangular data grids and form containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid Navy (`#1d4ed8`) text `#ffffff`, 4px radius, `font-weight: 500`. Hover state darkens smoothly to `#1e40af` with an active inset touch `transform: scale(0.99)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, text `#1e293b`. Hover: background `#f8fafc`, border `#94a3b8`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Borderless, text `#475569`. Hover: background `#f1f5f9`, text `#0f172a`.
- **Sizes:** Standard button height is compact at `36px` (vertical padding `6px`, horizontal padding `12px`); dense table utility buttons operate at `30px`.

### Badges & Status Indicators
Badges utilize a full-pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`) with subdued pastel backgrounds, matching hairline borders, and dark legible typography:
- **Resuelto / Validado:** `#ecfdf5` background, `#059669` text, `#a7f3d0` border.
- **En Revisión / Pendiente:** `#fffbeb` background, `#b45309` text, `#fde68a` border.
- **Crítico / Alerta:** `#fef2f2` background, `#b91c1c` text, `#fecaca` border.
- **Asignado / Informativo:** `#eff6ff` background, `#1d4ed8` text, `#bfdbfe` border.
- **Micro-Indicators:** 6px solid dots preceding the label denote real-time sync states.

### Data Tables (High Density)
- **Header:** `#f8fafc` background, text `#475569`, 11px uppercase `label-sm`, bottom border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`. Padding `8px 12px`.
- **Rows:** Height `40px` for standard density, `32px` for compact mode. Background `#ffffff`, separated by 1px solid `#f1f5f9`.
- **Row Interaction:** Hover: `#f8fafc`; Selected: `#eff6ff` with a left border accent of 2px solid `#1d4ed8`.
- **Monospace Cells:** Ticket/Incidence IDs (e.g., `QA2-2024-8841`) must use `data-mono` styling with color `#334155`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Containers:** 36px height, white background, 1px border `#cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`. Placeholder `#94a3b8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#1d4ed8`, box-shadow `0 0 0 2px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.
- **Validation:** 1px border `#dc2626`, accompanying helper text in 12px `#dc2626`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `16px x 16px`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, radius 3px for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Checked: background `#1d4ed8`, border `#1d4ed8`, displaying a white check icon.

### Cards & Audit Summary Containers
- White `#ffffff` background, 1px perimeter border `#e2e8f0`, radius 4px.
- Metric cards incorporate a thin `#f1f5f9` top status bar or a color-coded left border accent (3px) corresponding to the aggregated KPI state.
